🔗 Recommended External Tools

Curated tools, learning resources, and references I personally use and recommend to planning engineers.

🎓
Standards

PMI — Project Management Institute

The global standard for project management. Home of PMBOK Guide, PMP® certification, and project management research. Essential reference for every planning professional.

🔗 Visit pmi.org
⚖️
Standards

SCL Delay & Disruption Protocol

The Society of Construction Law's authoritative protocol on delay analysis and disruption claims. Required reading for anyone working on EOT claims. Free download available.

🔗 Visit scl.org.uk
💰
Standards

AACE International

Association for the Advancement of Cost Engineering. Home of Recommended Practices for EVM, forensic schedule analysis, and risk management (RP-68R-11). Essential reference body.

🔗 Visit aacei.org
🇧🇭
Regulatory

CRPEP Bahrain

Council for Regulating the Practice of Engineering Professions in the Kingdom of Bahrain. Mandatory registration for engineers working in Bahrain. Apply for RE status here.

🔗 Visit crpep.bh
💻
Software

Oracle Primavera P6

The industry-standard scheduling tool for EPC, oil & gas, and major infrastructure projects. Documentation, training resources, and licensing information available on Oracle's official site.

🔗 Visit oracle.com
📊
Software

Microsoft Power BI

The leading business intelligence tool for building executive dashboards from project data. Free desktop version for individuals — perfect for planning engineers building KPI dashboards.

🔗 Visit powerbi.microsoft.com

Learning

Plan Academy

Excellent online training platform specifically for Primavera P6, EVM, and project controls. Run by Michael Lepage — practical, project-focused content for working planners.

🔗 Visit planacademy.com

The leading construction and infrastructure news source for the GCC region. Project announcements, contractor news, and market intelligence relevant to GCC planning engineers.

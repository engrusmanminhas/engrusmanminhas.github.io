AI for project controls

Practical AI workflows I build to take the grind out of planning and controls — turning raw site inputs, cost workbooks and schedules into checked, report-ready outputs in minutes, not days. A human always reviews before anything is issued.

📝
Reporting

DPR Generator

Converts raw site inputs — progress notes, manpower counts, equipment logs — into a clean, formatted Daily Progress Report ready for client submittal.

Input

Site notes, manpower, equipment, weather, photos

→
AI process

Structures, summarises & flags missing fields

→
Output

Formatted DPR + carry-forward to WPR

🔁
Assurance

WPR vs DPR Checker

Verifies that the activities planned in the weekly programme actually match what the daily reports show on the ground — and surfaces the gaps.

Input

Weekly plan (WPR) + the week's daily reports (DPR)

→
AI process

Reconciles planned vs actual activity by activity

→
Output

Variance list + slippage & catch-up flags

📑
Commercial

Contract Scope Verifier

Checks additional or instructed site works against the contract scope, so potential variations and claims are spotted early instead of absorbed silently.

Input

Contract scope / BOQ + site instruction or extra work

→
AI process

Matches against scope; classifies in/out of contract

→
Output

Variation candidates + clause references to review

🧹
Cost

Budget Workbook Cleaner

Cleans and restructures messy cost-control workbooks — consolidating cost codes, fixing inconsistencies and shaping the data so dashboards can read it.

Input

Raw / inconsistent cost workbook

→
AI process

Normalises codes, dedupes, validates totals

→
Output

Clean, dashboard-ready cost dataset

🔍
Schedule quality

Schedule Audit Assistant

Reviews a P6 schedule for logic, links and resource assumptions — flagging open ends, negative float, constraints and broken logic against DCMA-style checks.

Input

P6 export (XER / XLSX) of the schedule

→
AI process

Runs logic / float / constraint health checks

→
Output

Audit report with prioritised fixes

A note on responsible use: these workflows assist a qualified planning engineer — they do not replace one. Every output is reviewed by a human before it is issued, and no confidential client data is shared in the samples shown across this site.
